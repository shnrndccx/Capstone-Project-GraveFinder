Our History

Rooted in Legacy

1978

The Beginning

TAPAT Park Developers, Inc. was formally organized, registered with the SEC, and began commercial operations as Garden of Memories Memorial Park. Drawn by the vision of Engr. Tomas Sanchez, Jr., the project was backed by prominent investors from Taguig and Pateros. Despite facing a major flood from two typhoons in its first year, the company's resolve was strengthened, leading to the decision to raise the entire park to a higher level.

1981

A Landmark Rises

A new chapel-office building was constructed in the middle of the park, which turned out to be the country’s biggest at the time—another first for Garden of Memories.

1993

Expanding Services

After fifteen years of success, the company ventured into pre-need services, establishing The Garden of Memories Memorial Life Plan Inc. to educate the public on the importance of planning for the inevitable.

1994

A Union of Companies

The stockholders approved the merger of TAPAT Park Developers Inc. and Garden of Memories Life Plan Inc., creating the new entity: Garden of Memories Memorial Park and Life Plan Inc. (GMMPLPI). This led to the construction of what was lauded as the country’s most elegant memorial chapel and mortuary complex.

2003

A Tradition of Caring

Celebrating its 25th anniversary, the company upheld its integrity by voluntarily surrendering its license to sell pre-need plans, protecting its name amidst industry-wide scandals.

2011

Commitment to Excellence

Major renovations were undertaken to upgrade park facilities, including the construction of new comfort rooms, reinforcing the company's commitment to visitor convenience and safety.

2018

Precious as Ruby

Celebrating its 40th Anniversary, GMMPCI embarked on the construction of a new Columbarium and Chapel Complex to meet the modern challenges of the community, continuing its legacy of innovation and service.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the park hours?

The memorial park is open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Our main office is open from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

How do I locate a loved one?

You can use the search tool on our homepage. Enter the name and relationship to view the exact location and map instructions.

Do I need an appointment?

While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure a counselor is available to assist you with memorial services or inquiries.

Rules & Regulations

Visitor Guidelines

All Saints’ Day Celebration - Important Reminders

To remember with fondness the heroes and saints of our lives and for an orderly, peaceful celebration of All Saints’ / Souls’ Day, please be reminded of the following. We like to enjoin everyone’s support and cooperation:

  1. On October 31 and November 1 starting at 6:00 a.m., Bagong Calzada will be one-way traffic going to Pasig (northbound). Vehicles from Pasig going to Garden of Memories will pass through M. Almeda St. (2-way traffic), left to Tomas Ave., left to Bagong Calzada.
  2. Main gate will be used for ENTRY of vehicles from October 31 – November 1. EXIT of vehicles will be at Greenville Gate, towards Pateros proper or Taguig. All vehicles and pedestrians entering the Park will be subject to security check for safety purposes. Please note that parking space is limited inside the Park.
  3. MAIN GATE WILL BE CLOSED FOR ENTRY OF VEHICLES ON NOVEMBER 1 FROM 12:00 NN TO 8:00 P.M.
  4. Pedestrian’s Entry and Exit:
    • Main Gate and Greenville Gate - October 31 (6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.)
    • Main Gate - November 1 (6:00 a.m. to 12:00 m.n.)
    • Gate at Court of St. Lazarus – Nov. 1 (6:00 a.m. onwards)
    • Greenville Gate – November 1 (6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.)
  5. All pedestrians and their belongings will be subject to security frisking upon entry.
  6. Tents and its accessories should not encroach other people’s property (gravesite). The Park is not responsible for loss or damage of tents installed and left unattended. All tents must be removed on or before November 4 from 6:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Otherwise, Park personnel will remove the tents for safekeeping. Applicable storage fees will apply and will be released only upon proper identification.
  7. Please give courtesy to other people’s graves by not covering their flat markers (lapida).
  8. The following items are NOT ALLOWED to be brought inside the Park which may disturb the solemnity of the occasion:
    • Liquor and other intoxicating drinks
    • Household appliances and gadgets (even battery operated); generators
    • Bicycles, motorcycles, hoppers, pedicabs, scooters, rollerblades, e-bike and the like
    • Pets
    STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Violators will be apprehended and/or turned over to Police authorities:
    • Prohibited drugs
    • Firearms, knives and other deadly weapons – these should be turned over to the Security Guard and PNP personnel on duty during inspection and to be claimed upon getting out. Otherwise, these will be confiscated and the one caught possessing any of these items may be investigated and turned-over to police;
    • Any form of gambling
    • Drinking of liquor and other intoxicating drinks
    • Ambulant vendors
    • Lighting candles and/or candle dripping, flowers from other tombs or graves
    • Double parking and parking at unauthorized areas (any vehicle can be towed or issued citation tickets by traffic enforcers)
    • Cooking/grilling on gravesites
    • Solicitation of any kind
    • Boisterous laughter, loud noise
    • Putting of fences, straws, ropes or any temporary markings at gravesites as this prevents people to move easily. These will be removed by our Park personnel.
    • Fire cracker, Fireworks and the likes
    • Violation of other Park Rules and Regulations
  9. Garden of Memories supports the government regulation on smoking. Please smoke at the designated smoking areas.
  10. HOLY MASS SCHEDULES at Park Chapel: November 1 – 10:00 A.M. and 4:00 P.M.
  11. Volunteers from the Philippine National Red Cross and Fil-Chinese Organization will be stationed on October 31 and November 1 at Park Chapel grounds to administer First Aid when needed.
  12. If possible OBSERVE SOCIAL DISTANCING.
  13. Please take care of your children and your personal belongings. The Park will not be responsible for any loss or damaged items brought inside the Park.
  14. Report to us immediately (at Main Building / Police) any suspicious looking persons or bags left indiscriminately.
  15. Please dispose garbage properly. Garbage collectors will be on hand to collect your garbage.
  16. One-Way Traffic Flow inside the Park will be effected from October 31 & November 1.
  17. To all senior citizens who will visit their gravesite, always bring your maintenance medicine and to all babies’ parents bring their allergy medicine for their protection. Handicapped persons/seniors are advised to visit their gravesite on October 31 & November 2.
